# GreenShift Campaign Video Script

**Speakers:** Kiyarash Shahriari Kesheh, Nikita Petylo, Adrian Mendoza
**Setting:** Outdoors, surrounded by trees and bushes, natural daylight
**Tone:** Passionate, formal, data-driven
**Estimated length:** 2 to 3 minutes

---

## HOW TO FILM

- Film outside near trees or a park. No backgrounds with buildings or roads if possible.
- Each person speaks directly to the camera.
- Wear clean, solid-colored clothing. Avoid busy patterns.
- Film in good daylight. Avoid harsh midday sun directly overhead — morning or late afternoon light looks best.
- Each person can stand slightly offset from center to look more natural.
- Keep a steady shot. Use a tripod or prop the phone against something solid.

**When you upload the finished video, name the file exactly:**
```
greenshift-campaign.mp4
```
**And place it in this folder in the repository:**
```
assets/videos/greenshift-campaign.mp4
```
The website will automatically display it once the file is there.

## FULL SCRIPT

---

### KIYARASH — Founder and CEO

*(Opens the video, speaks with confidence and urgency)*

Hi. My name is Kiyarash Shahriari Kesheh, and I am the Founder and CEO of GreenShift.

Every single day, our cities lose more green space. We are living through record heat waves. Our rivers and oceans are choking on plastic. And our forests, the lungs of this planet, are being cut down faster than they can grow back.

At GreenShift, we believe this does not have to be our future.

We plant native trees and living walls to cool neighborhoods by up to ten degrees Fahrenheit. We send volunteers to remove plastic from waterways. And we fight to stop deforestation before it is too late.

Every project we run cuts an average of 2,000 metric tons of CO2 and helps bring vital ecosystems back to life.

But we cannot do this without you.

*(Pause — look directly into the camera)*

Join us.

---

### NIKITA — Treasurer

*(Steps in, warm and community-focused)*

My name is Nikita Petylo, and I am the Treasurer at GreenShift.

What makes this organization different is that we do not just talk about change. We organize it. I lead the fundraisers and community events that turn our goals into real action on the ground.

You can get involved right now. Download our free carbon tracking app. Sign up for a tree planting event in your area. Or donate to help us bring GreenShift to more cities and more countries.

No contribution is too small. Every tree planted, every piece of plastic removed, every dollar raised — it all adds up.

*(Smile, genuine)*

Together, we can paint the world green.

---

### ADRIAN — Chief Engineer

*(Energetic, forward-looking)*

My name is Adrian Mendoza. I am the Chief Engineer at GreenShift, and I built the GreenShift carbon tracking app.

Today that app has over 500,000 active users around the world.

With it, you can measure your own carbon footprint, discover green events near you, and watch in real time how much CO2 your choices are saving.

We built this because we believe data drives change. When people can actually see the impact of what they do, they do more of it.

GreenShift is not just a vision. It is a real, measurable solution to climate change. Grounded in science. Powered by community.

*(Look directly into camera, strong finish)*

And it starts with you.

---

### CLOSING — All three together or back to Kiyarash

*(Can film individually and cut together, or stand together for the final line)*

**Join GreenShift.**
**Download the app.**
**Plant a tree.**
**Together, let us paint the world green.**

---

*End of script.*
