# Unplug Collective Campaign Video Script

**Speakers:** Kiyanoosh Shahriari Kesheh, Sean Khirich, Daniel McConnaughy
**Setting:** Outdoors, park or area with trees and natural light
**Tone:** Conversational, honest, direct
**Estimated length:** 2 to 3 minutes

---

## HOW TO FILM

- Film outside near trees, a park, or somewhere with natural greenery.
- Each person speaks directly to the camera.
- Wear clean, solid-colored clothing. Avoid busy patterns.
- Film in good natural daylight. Morning or late afternoon light looks best.
- Each person can stand slightly off center to look more natural.
- Keep a steady shot. Use a tripod or prop the phone against something solid.

**When you upload the finished video, name the file exactly:**
```
unplug-campaign.mp4
```
**And place it in this folder in the repository:**
```
assets/videos/unplug-campaign.mp4
```
The website will display it automatically once the file is there.

## FULL SCRIPT

---

### KIYANOOSH — Web Developer, Team Member

*(Opens the video, speaks with confidence)*

Hi. My name is Kiyanoosh Shahriari Kesheh, and I am part of The Unplug Collective.

The average American teenager spends more than 7 hours a day on a screen, not counting school. That is nearly half of every waking hour handed over to apps designed by engineers whose only goal is to keep you scrolling.

We are not here to say technology is bad. We are here to say that your attention belongs to you. And right now, most of us have given it away without even noticing.

The Unplug Collective is asking one simple thing: give yourself one hour offline each day. One hour to be present. Science shows that even a small break from screens lowers your stress, improves your focus, and makes your mood better within just two weeks.

*(Pause, look directly into camera)*

You deserve a mind that belongs to you. Join us.

---

### SEAN — Team Member

*(Warm, community-focused)*

My name is Sean Khirich, and I am a member of The Unplug Collective.

What makes this campaign different is that we are not telling you what to do. We are sharing what the research actually says and letting you decide for yourself.

Studies show that teens who spend more than three hours a day on social media face a much higher risk of depression, anxiety, and sleep problems. The Surgeon General of the United States has warned parents and teenagers about this directly. The evidence is real.

But here is the good news. Teens who cut back by even 30 minutes a day report better moods and higher self esteem in just a few weeks. You do not have to quit. You just have to be intentional.

*(Smile, genuine)*

Scroll less. Live more.

---

### DANIEL — Team Member

*(Energetic, forward-looking)*

My name is Daniel McConnaughy, and I am part of The Unplug Collective.

Think about what you could do with 7 hours. That is enough time to read, create, exercise, have a real conversation, or learn something new. Instead, most of us spend it chasing a scroll that never ends.

The Unplug Collective is not about fear. It is about choice. When you know what your phone is doing to your brain, and when you actually feel the difference that one offline hour makes, you start making different decisions.

*(Look directly into camera, strong finish)*

It starts with one hour. And it starts today.

---

### CLOSING — All three together or back to Kiyanoosh

*(Can film individually and cut together, or stand together for the final line)*

**Join The Unplug Collective.**
**Put the phone down.**
**Take one hour back.**
**Scroll less. Live more.**

---

*End of script.*
